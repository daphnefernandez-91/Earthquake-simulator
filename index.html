🌍 What is an earthquake?

An earthquake happens when energy is suddenly released inside the Earth, causing the ground to shake.

🔎 What changes the damage?

  • How deep the earthquake starts
  • How far away the town is
  • Whether the ground is soft or firm
  • How strong and well-built the buildings are

💡 Remember!

Magnitude alone does NOT determine the damage.

Two earthquakes with the same magnitude can cause very different damage in different places.
